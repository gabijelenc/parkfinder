# parkFinder — Driver Design System v0.1
Status: user-approved design direction and functional decisions 1–32; team review, implementation and acceptance pending. Mockups are illustrative, not evidence of working functionality. Do not include real personal data from mockups in public repo.

## Brand
parkFinder (case-sensitive), tagline “Tu lugar, antes de llegar.” Personality: smart technology, premium experience accessible to everyone. Isotype: simple front-facing vehicle inside parking bay, original SVG, scalable and accessible. Urban Tech palette: navy #0B1220, graphite #334155, mint #34D399, light background #F8FAFC, white #FFFFFF. Inter typography with system fallback. Light/dark manual toggle persisted locally; no automatic mode required. Mint primary actions with dark text, contrast AA; unavailable states also use text/icons, not color alone. Linear icons, 8px spacing, 44px minimum touch targets, responsive and keyboard accessible.

## Driver experience — decisions approved
Desktop: hybrid map/list, default 60% map / 40% list with adjustable divider, compact integrated search (destination, date/arrival/departure, vehicle). Mobile: full-screen map, top “¿A dónde vas?” progressive search and draggable accessible results sheet. Search supports address, POI, businesses, current location by explicit permission, recent and favorite destinations; demo catalog/offline fallback; no paid geocoding. Keep date/time/vehicle constraints and technical QA separate. Header/navigation: Explorar, Mis reservas, Favoritos, Mi perfil; mobile bottom navigation.
Map pins: total price, available mint/unavailable gray, selected outline; sync card and pin. Cards: illustrative labeled photo, name/address, demo rating/review count, estimated straight-line walk distance/time, availability, services, total and average hourly price, compatible free capacity for entire interval, favorite and reserve. Quick controls cheapest/nearest/top rated, covered, 24h; advanced combined filters (price, distance, rating, CCTV/security, accessibility, EV, valet). Recommendations: lowest total, closest, highest demo rating, value price-distance, low availability (no false urgency); formulas explicit and testable. Proposed value score normalize price/distance among eligible results, equal weights; low availability threshold <=2 actual synthetic free bays; team review.
Detail: desktop side panel/mobile full screen; main photo, thumbnails, expandable gallery, all illustrative labels. Tabs Info, Servicios, Fotos, Reseñas, Ubicación. Include hours, height, allowed vehicle types, tri-state amenities, demo reviews, directions; price total/average/hourly expandable breakdown. Compare up to 3 garages with same interval and vehicle, price/distance/rating/capacity/services/hours.
Checkout: review → payment choice → confirmation; choose saved vehicle/default or edit; multiple vehicles (plate, make, model, type, color, height where applicable). Browse freely, account required at booking; local demo persona only until backend/auth, no real passwords or personal information, Google/Apple/email sign-in future. Payment online or at garage according to policy, simulated only, no real card inputs or payment claims. Confirmation: success, reservation code, QR (demo-only until operator validates), address, dates, vehicle, locked total, Google Maps directions, calendar .ics, share, cancellation terms. Extension: validate interval capacity/operating hours and new quote, explicit acceptance before update; retain history. Notifications individually configurable: confirmation, 1h prior, 30m before end, extension offer, exit, review; local/in-app simulation only, no promise background push.
My bookings: active, upcoming, completed, cancelled; active prominent QR, time remaining, extend; completed only by explicit simulated operational events. Favorites: garages and destinations. Profile: demo name/contact, vehicles, payment preference only, notifications, light/dark, ES/EN, privacy/help. Personal analytics monthly spending, booking count, parked hours, frequent garages, payment history, export (CSV now; PDF/XLSX future); distinguish booked estimated from simulated paid/actual parked.
