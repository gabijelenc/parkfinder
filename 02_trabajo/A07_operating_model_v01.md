# A07 — Operations & Delivery Model · v0.1 · draft
Five guiding questions (Class 2, p. 3):
1. Delivery: search, compare, revalidate, confirm, admit, close, reconcile.
2. People: driver submits accurate request; garage manager maintains capacity/rates/hours; attendant validates arrival/departure; platform support handles exceptions; technology monitors dependencies; finance reconciles payments when enabled.
3. Resources: participating bays, capacity records, browser/app, booking store, map adapter, support channels, trained attendants, future payment provider.
4. Failure: stale inventory → reject or suspend new bookings and reconcile; map outage → list/manual destination fallback; admission conflict → verify reference, alternative/refund review; payment uncertainty → pending status, reconcile before confirmation.
5. Improvement: review booking failures, inventory mismatches, no-shows and complaints weekly; assign corrective action and verify next period.
Lifecycle: onboard garage and validate restrictions; activate after data review; operate bookings and attendance; monitor daily; resolve incidents; review trends and adjust approved rates/capacity. Human approval required for price recommendations and dispute outcomes.
Handoff: operator publishes capacity → platform validates quote → driver receives reference → attendant validates admission → owner sees operational record. Critical dependency: timely and accurate capacity updates. Prototype has no real partner contract, staffing or service-level guarantee.
