# ParkFinder — Product brief · v0.1 · draft
Source: Initial Product Concept, slides 2–7; subsequent proposed refinements.

**Problem:** Urban drivers traveling to a high-demand or unfamiliar destination lack reliable, comparable parking availability and total-cost information before arrival.
**User:** Driver; secondary users are garage operators and owners.
**Design challenge:** How might we help drivers secure suitable parking before arrival, given limited and potentially stale capacity information?
**Concept:** Map-based discovery, comparison and advance reservation at participating private garages in Buenos Aires.
**Expected value (hypotheses):** Less search time, predictable arrival, price transparency, higher utilization and an additional sales channel for operators. Not measured yet.
**Core capability:** Match a destination, vehicle and time interval to eligible test capacity, confirm a booking and prevent local overselling.
**Typical use case:** Driver plans an appointment, compares nearby garages, sees price and constraints, books a time window and receives a reference.
**Before:** Search multiple locations, ask about vacancies, drive without certainty.
**After:** Compare options and price, validate test availability, reserve before departure.
**Scope:** Demonstrate with synthetic inventory; live occupancy, real payments, physical admission and cross-device synchronization are not currently established.
**Alternatives to validate:** Directory only, phone-based reservation, fixed pricing platform. Proposed choice: interval-based reservation because availability certainty is central.
