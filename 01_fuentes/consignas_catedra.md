# Academic requirements — v0 · 2026-11-25
Source: MBA Course Guidelines & Deliverables, pp. 4–13; Class 1, pp. 8–15; Class 2, pp. 3, 10, 12–19; Workshop, pp. 21–25. This is a team-oriented paraphrase, not a reproduction of the course slides.

| Dimension | Weight | Evidence |
|---|---:|---|
| Prototype, Functionality & Iteration | 30% | One working core capability; V1, V2, V3; two internal reviews; limitations explicitly labeled |
| Problem, Need & Product Value | 15% | Problem statement, target user, typical use case and before/after comparison |
| Technology & Architecture | 15% | High-level diagram, components, data flows, rationale and integration status |
| Operations & Delivery | 20% | End-to-end workflow, owners, dependencies, exception response and relevant KPIs |
| Risk & Governance | 20% | 3–5 material risks, likelihood/impact, gross/net scores, controls, accountable roles and escalation |

Four final deliverables: accessible V3 PoC; approximately one-page Iteration & Decision Log; 10–12 slide executive presentation; concise technology, operations and risk documentation. Production readiness, full integrations, market sizing and formal BPMN are not mandatory. Service Blueprint is recommended, not mandatory. Internal backlog is a working aid, not an extra submission. Oral defense assesses the same five dimensions. All members must be able to defend decisions.
